About Me

Matthew graduated from the University of Nottingham in 2025 with a Mathematics MMath. He spent the second half of his degree specialising in theoretical physics, with his final year project investigating the eigenvalue statistics of chaotic quantum systems and their connection to Random Matrix Theory. He's excited to continue his study of PDEs as well as explore probability and scientific computation at SAMBa. Master's Project: Quantum Properties of Chaotic Systems

    Developed a Julia-based Boundary Element Method framework for computing eigenmodes of two-dimensional quantum billiard systems governed by the Helmholtz equation. The framework enabled statistical analysis of resonant spectra and wave-function visualisation across a range of geometries. Efficient kernel tabulation and multi-threaded matrix assembly were implemented to improve the efficiency of large-scale computations.
