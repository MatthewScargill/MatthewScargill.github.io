About Me

Matthew graduated from the University of Nottingham in 2025 with a Mathematics MMath. He spent the second half of his degree specialising in theoretical physics, with his final year project investigating the eigenvalue statistics of chaotic quantum systems and their connection to Random Matrix Theory. He's excited to continue his study of PDEs as well as explore probability and scientific computation at SAMBa. Beyond maths, Matthew enjoys cooking, rowing, and sea shanties (clearly a pirate in another life). His favourite karaoke song is "Holding Out for a Hero" by Bonnie Tyler.
